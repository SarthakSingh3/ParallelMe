# Prior-Art & Comparative Landscape

ParallelMe does not claim global novelty. It operates at the intersection of established disciplines.

| Category | Typical Tools | Capabilities | What ParallelMe Does Differently |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Probabilistic Spreadsheets** | Guesstimate, Causal.app | General Monte Carlo simulation on arbitrary user equations. | ParallelMe enforces structured decision schemas, shared capacity constraint validation across alternative options, and empirical observation tracking. |
| **Enterprise Decision Support** | TreeAge, DPL, Expert Choice | Decision tree evaluation, expected value, multi-attribute utility. | ParallelMe is open-source, student/individual focused, runs entirely locally, and emphasizes real-time observation logging against simulated ranges. |
| **Financial Monte Carlo** | Portfolio Visualizer, cFIREsim | Simulates retirement survival probabilities from historical asset return bootstrap distributions. | ParallelMe focuses on personal skill acquisition and learning trade-offs with non-linear diminishing returns and schedule volatility. |
| **Habit & Quantified Self** | Beeminder, Exist.io, Toggl | Logs empirical tracking, streak enforcement, and backward-looking charts. | ParallelMe adds forward-looking counterfactual simulation under uncertainty prior to commitment. |
